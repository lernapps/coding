Coding workshop

You program with blocks. A robot runs your program, step by step. For year 7 and 8 (age 12 to 14).

Let's go

Choose

  • Maze 1: Around the corner
  • Maze 2: The stairs
  • Maze 3: Follow the corridor
  • Turtle 1: A square
  • Turtle 2: Square with a loop
  • Turtle 3: The spirograph
  • Build your own task For teachers and apps that send the workshop a task as a link.

How it works

  • Choose a task.
  • Drag blocks onto the work area and snap them together. With the keyboard: Tab to the blocks, Enter adds a block.
  • Press “Run”. With “One step” you see each command on its own.
  • Your program has to work in every test case.

What you learn

  • I can put instructions in the right order to make a sequence.
  • I can use a count-controlled loop to run instructions several times.
  • I can use a condition-controlled loop to run instructions until a condition is met.
  • I can use a selection (if – then – else) to choose what happens depending on a condition.
